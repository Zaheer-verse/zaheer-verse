## Selected Projects

### 01 — Eye-Blink Smart Home System
**Assistive Technology · IoT · Embedded Systems**

<img width="100%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Zaheer-verse/zaheer-verse/main/app/public/smart-home-hero.svg" alt="Eye-Blink Smart Home System"/>

Hands-free home automation designed for users with limited mobility. Wearable infrared sensors detect intentional eye-blink events. A NodeMCU ESP8266 processes the signals and controls appliances through relays under defined safety constraints.

### 02 — EV Energy Harvesting
**Electric Vehicles · Wireless Power · Energy Systems**

<img width="100%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Zaheer-verse/zaheer-verse/main/app/public/ev-harvesting-hero.svg" alt="EV Energy Harvesting"/>

Research concept for intelligent road infrastructure capable of transferring and harvesting energy while electric vehicles are in motion. Integrates dynamic resonant inductive charging with thermal and vibration energy recovery from the roadway.

### 03 — DesignForge
**AI Tooling · Interface Engineering · Open Source**

<img width="100%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Zaheer-verse/zaheer-verse/main/app/public/designforge-hero.svg" alt="DesignForge"/>

Structured design-engineering workflow for AI coding agents. Enforces explicit reasoning on product intent, visual direction, accessibility, and responsiveness before any front-end code is generated.

### 04 — Deliberate Dev (Codex & Claude)
**AI Agents · Test-Driven Development · Process Automation**

<img width="49%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Zaheer-verse/zaheer-verse/main/app/public/deliberate-codex-hero.svg" alt="Deliberate Dev Codex"/>
<img width="49%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Zaheer-verse/zaheer-verse/main/app/public/deliberate-claude-hero.svg" alt="Deliberate Dev Claude"/>

Software delivery workflow plugin for AI coding agents (Codex and Claude Code). Converts ambiguous prompts into a controlled sequence: requirements clarification, test-first implementation, worktree isolation, engineering review, and verification.

### 05 — Expense Management System
**React · TypeScript · Financial Interface**

<img width="100%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Zaheer-verse/zaheer-verse/main/app/public/expense-management-hero.svg" alt="Expense Management System"/>

Personal finance application for tracking expenses, defining budget limits, and reviewing monthly insights through a clean, responsive dashboard interface.
